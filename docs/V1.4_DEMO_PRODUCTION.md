# ARGOS PM v1.4.0 — one-minute product demo production brief

**Status:** Revised at the user's request on 20 October 2026: 60 seconds instead of 30, no music, normal-speed excerpts, a Warnings correction story, and editorial titles clearly outside the application. See [verification notes](V1.4_DEMO_VERIFICATION.md), including the native picker limitation. Preserve `docs/media/pm-poc-30s-demo-v1.3.0.mp4` unchanged as historical footage.

## Objective and capture rules

Create a silent 60-second 1920×1080 MP4 from **real ARGOS PM v1.4.0 interactions**. Use a disposable local `.pmp` project with fictional names and no private data. Do not overwrite an existing user project or fabricate UI interactions. Show normal-speed excerpts; shorten repeated setup with straight cuts rather than time compression. Hold result states long enough to read.

Use an unscaled 1840×864 application viewport inside a 1920×1080 editorial canvas. Place chapter titles above the framed application, on a warm cream background with dark text and a coral “PRODUCT DEMO” label. Keep titles outside product pixels so viewers can distinguish the video presentation from application chrome. Introduce ARGOS PM v1.4.0 immediately and retain the branding throughout.

## Creative rationale

The design applies current [LinkedIn video guidance](https://business.linkedin.com/advertise/ads/sponsored-content/video-ads/tips) on visual storytelling and sound-off comprehension, and [Google Ads' ABCD guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14783551?hl=en) on early branding, focused messages, contrast and a clear next step. The editorial header and frame are design choices for this demo; the product interface remains unchanged.
