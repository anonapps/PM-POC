## Scope decision

A full task editing panel remains an outstanding product clarification; inline editing and quick edits remain intact pending that answer.

Validation and screenshot evidence will be appended after execution. Native Windows/macOS picker consent and physical disk acceptance cannot be established by Linux browser file adapters. No merge, deploy, tag or release is authorized.
